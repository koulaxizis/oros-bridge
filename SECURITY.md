# Security policy

Thank you for helping keep orOS and its users safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately, not in a public issue:

- e-mail **koulaxizis@glarolykoi.net** with the subject `Security: oros-bridge`, or
- if this repository's Security tab offers **Report a vulnerability**, use that.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, which version or commit you used, and what an attacker could do with it. English or Greek are both fine.

## What happens next

- We acknowledge your report within 7 days.
- We keep you informed while we work on it, and agree a date for publishing the details with you.
- We fix the problem in the newest release. Older versions are not patched; the update path is the fix.
- If you wish, we credit you in the release notes.

We do not take legal action against good-faith research that respects people's privacy: test only against your own data and devices, do not access or keep other people's data, and do not degrade the service for others (no denial-of-service, spam or social engineering).

## Scope

This repository covers orOS Bridge: the WordPress plugin and its connection to orOS.

orOS is free software made by one person; there is no paid bug bounty.

The same contact is published in [useoros.online/.well-known/security.txt](https://useoros.online/.well-known/security.txt).
